# smoke-game
This is a game about Wizards (using the power of smoke) versus Humans who hate them, and those who suffer from magical enchantments
cast upon them.

# What is the game about?
This game is an RPG in which people and wizards exist.
People are physically stronger, but cannot release their own smoke (they can't cast magic).
Wizards are physically weaker, but can use their genetic ability and their smoke to cast magical spells from different schools of magic.
Wizards commonly practice on human victims; therefore, most humans despise wizards.
Additionally, wizards are usually wealthier due to their powers.

# The human victims
When a wizard casts a transformation school of magic spell on a human, the human takes the form of a cat, a dog, or a lizard.
Such victims maintain their human shape and form, but inherit animal-like abilities (and disabilities!).

# A bit about the player
The player can start out as a human, a wizard, or a victim of magic.
A player playing as a human or wizard may at any point be turned into a victim of magic. In such a case, they have 5 days to cure themselves before more important matters need to happen.

# What is the point of the game?
The game is an RPG; therefore, it has many quests and several questlines, each involving their own characters and tasks.

The questline stories are not yet decided.

## Technologies used
