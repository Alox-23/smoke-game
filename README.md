# smoke-game
This is a game about Wizards (using the power of smoke) versus Humans who hate them, and those who suffer from magical encatacions 
cast uppon them.

# What is the game about?
This game is an RPG in which exist people and wizards. 

People are physically stronger, but cannot release their own smoke (they cant cast magic)

Wizards are physically weaker, but can use their geneitc ability and their smoke to cast magical spells from different schools of magic.

Wizard commonly practice on human victims, therefore, most humans dispise wizards. 

Additionaly, wizards are usualy wealthier due to their powers. 

# The human victims
When a wizard hasts a transoframtion school of magic spell on a human, the human takes the form of a cat, a dog, or a lizard.

Such victims maintain their human shape and form, but inherit animal-like abilities (and disabilities!)

# A bit about the player

The player can start out as a human, a wizard or a vicim of magic. 

A player playing as a human or wizard may at any point be turned into a victim of magic. In such case they have 5 days to cure themselves before more important matters need to happen. 

# What is the point of the game?

The game is an RPG, therefore it has many quests and sevral questlines, involving their own characters and tasks.
 
The questline stories are not yet decided.
